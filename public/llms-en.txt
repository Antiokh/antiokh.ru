# Anton Vladislavovich Nazarov (Antiokh)

> Official personal website of Anton Vladislavovich Nazarov, known as Antiokh. Technology leader, systems architect, and hands-on builder based in Belgrade, Serbia. Working professionally in IT since 2008.

Anton began as a junior Lotus Notes developer at Domodedovo Airport. His work expanded from enterprise document systems and internal development into process analysis, systems architecture, implementation, and IT leadership. He built an internal bank document-management system, developed an industry information platform used across a network of 100+ cultural institutions, helped establish the IT function of a new organization, and later ran IT at ZIL Cultural Center.

After moving to Belgrade in 2022, Anton returned to intensive hands-on delivery and independent client work. Through NeedleBit he designs and builds ERP systems, internal tools, client portals, automation, integrations, and AI-enabled products at the intersection of business processes, data, architecture, and implementation.

The recurring pattern in his work is to understand the real operating process, draw clear system boundaries, make the result usable in production, and leave enough structure and documentation for another qualified person to continue it after the first release.
