# Anton Vladislavovich Nazarov (Antiokh)

> Official personal website of Anton Vladislavovich Nazarov, known as Antiokh. Based in Belgrade, Serbia. Working professionally in IT since 2008.

Anton began as a junior Lotus Notes developer at Domodedovo Airport. His work gradually expanded from enterprise development into process analysis, systems architecture, implementation, and IT leadership. He worked in banking and public-sector environments, developed an industry information platform used across a network of more than 100 institutions, and later served as Head of IT at ZIL Cultural Center. After moving to Belgrade in 2022, he returned to intensive hands-on product building and developed the independent practice that became NeedleBit.

The recurring theme in his work is systems that sit between technology, people, and real operations: ERP, internal platforms, workflow automation, field operations, assessment products, and AI-assisted engineering.

Outside work, Anton dances hustle / discofox, makes music, and has organized events. He plays several instruments and is interested in photography, design, psychology, philosophy, cinema, and travel.
