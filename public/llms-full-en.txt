## Summary

Anton Nazarov is a technology leader, systems architect, project/product manager, and hands-on builder. His background spans enterprise systems, banking, public-sector and cultural institutions, IT-function creation, Head of IT responsibility, client delivery, ERP, internal platforms, automation, and AI-assisted engineering.

His recurring delivery pattern is to reconstruct the real operating process and human ownership first; define data, roles, permissions, integrations, and system boundaries next; and then carry the result through implementation, production use, user adoption, documentation, and handover.
